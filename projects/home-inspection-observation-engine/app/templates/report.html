Overview
About This Report
{{ total_findings }} finding{{ "s" if total_findings != 1 else "" }} across {{ sections | length }} system{{ "s" if sections | length != 1 else "" }} — inspected {{ inspection_date_fmt }}

This report documents conditions observed at the property on {{ inspection_date_fmt }}. Findings are organized by building system and rated using a three-tier severity system to help you prioritize action. Every finding includes a recommended next step and a general cost magnitude. Finding IDs (e.g., §2.3) cross-reference the Priority Action Items table at the back of this report.

⚠
Safety Hazard
A condition that poses a risk of injury or harm to occupants. Immediate Safety items must be corrected before occupancy. Safety Upgrade items were legal at the time of construction but are now recognized code hazards.
🔧
Deficiency
A functional failure, significant expense, or condition requiring specialist evaluation. Sub-types: Major Repair (licensed specialist required), Repair / Replace (general contractor level), Evaluate (inspector could not fully assess — specialist needed), End of Life (functional but aging — budget for replacement).
ℹ
Advisory
Maintenance needs, monitoring items, and informational context. Maintenance items are routine upkeep that is overdue. Monitor items need watching but no action now. Informational items require no action — they provide context for the buyer.

Cost estimates are general magnitude approximations, not contractor quotes. Obtain professional bids before negotiating repairs. Satisfactory items are not written as findings — the absence of a finding for a component means no deficiency was observed. Items that could not be inspected are listed in the Not Inspected section.
