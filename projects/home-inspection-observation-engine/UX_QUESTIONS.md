# UX Questions for Inspector Testing

These are open design decisions that should be validated with real inspectors during early pilots.

## Audio Recording Interaction
**Current behavior:** Press and hold the record button to record; release to stop.
**Alternative:** Tap to start, tap again to stop.
**Why it matters:** Press-and-hold feels more intentional and reduces accidental long recordings. But tap-to-stop may be easier when the inspector has one hand occupied (holding a flashlight, pointing at a defect, etc.).
**Action:** Test both with a small group of inspectors before committing.
